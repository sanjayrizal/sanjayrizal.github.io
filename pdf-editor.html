Free Online PDF Editor

Edit text in a PDF, add your own text and images, highlight or white out areas, and download the result. Your file stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

How editing works: “Edit existing text” covers the original words with a box and puts your replacement on top, using Helvetica rather than the original font. The covered text is still inside the file, so do not use this to hide private information. Scanned PDFs have no text to select. Latin characters only; others export as “?”. Password-protected PDFs and rotated pages are not supported.
