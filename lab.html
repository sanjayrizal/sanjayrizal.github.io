Engineering Lab

Four small tools for preliminary electrical calculations. All maths runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. These are learning and first-pass estimates, not design sign-off.

Solar PV sizing

Preliminary grid-connected sizing from consumption, solar resource and available area.

Single & three-phase power

Real, apparent and reactive power from voltage, current and power factor.

Load energy analyzer

Estimate daily and monthly energy and cost from a list of loads. Example values only.

DC resistor circuit

A DC source with resistors purely in series or purely in parallel. This is not a general (SPICE-style) solver.
